# Before the First Pour / 첫 잔이 놓이기까지

Charim Stories · Episode 02 · Version 1

An original six-panel fictional maker narrative, English first with a Korean reading option. Haein and the workshop are invented. The images do not identify a manufacturer or establish Charim production specifications.

## Sequence

1. Preparing pale clay at the bench.
2. Shaping a small rounded tea cup on a wheel.
3. Trimming a leather-hard cup’s foot.
4. Developing a glaze, with dried/prepared or fired stages between panels.
5. Inspecting after the kiln has fully cooled.
6. Pausing with the completed concept pair after decoration and any required further finishing/firing.

The sequence compresses multiple days and is narrative art, not a technical ceramic tutorial. Fired glaze color and decoration routes require real samples.

## Visual direction

An original adult Korean woman ceramic maker, low black bun, ivory work blouse, rolled sleeves and black apron. A modest contemporary Korean workshop with hanok timber details. Clean white, ink black and pale celadon; subtle clay neutrals. Detailed graphic-novel illustration with dimensional ceramics. No palace costumes, warm paper wallpaper, magical transformations or in-image lettering. English/Korean dialogue is accessible HTML on the website.

## Deliverables

Three source PNG pages, each two panels; responsive WebP derivatives on the website; archive copies. `art-direction.json` records the complete prompt set and generation mode.
