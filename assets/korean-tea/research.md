# First bundle: 찻자리 / The Korean Tea Ritual

## Direction

A single prospective Korean tea bundle, with an ivory, jade and dusty plum visual palette. Contemporary Korean romantic atmosphere, original characters and scenes. The supplied botanical logo is preserved.

## Sources consulted October 26, 2026

- Asian Art Museum, [Korean Tea Ceremony](https://education.asianart.org/resources/korean-tea-ceremony/): tea master Youngmi Yi and scholar Jaesup Pak introduce cha and darye, with flexibility in enjoying tea. Supports the cultural foundation, not endorsement of the product.
- Encyclopedia of Korean Culture, [다도](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0013419): history and practices of attentive brewing and serving; pouring among cups for similar strength and noticing aroma and taste.

## Modern product proposal

Korean loose-leaf green tea, a porcelain side-handle pot, two cups, a cooling bowl, a wooden tray and bojagi-inspired cloth, plus an illustrated guide. Makers, tea quantities, materials, prices and shipping are not confirmed. Do not claim artisan production, royal provenance, museum endorsement or medical benefits.
