# Its Korean Roots: craft techniques

Researched 2026-10-27. New diagrams are original schematic SVGs in `app/korean-roots/craft-traditions.tsx`. They explain principles; no generated image is presented as documentary craft photography. No third-party images copied.

- Sagijang: National Intangible Heritage Center, 2023 public demonstration record. https://support.nihc.go.kr/iptl/event/eventF.do?MENU_TAB_SE=4&eventUid=169278196892016 . Clay purification in water, wheel forming, first firing, glaze, second firing. No universal firing temperature or specific Charim manufacturing claim.
- Celadon: Soyoung Lee, The Met, Goryeo Celadon (2003). https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/goryeo-celadon . Raw materials, glaze, reducing atmosphere; sanggam incised clay filled with slips before glaze/firing. Distinguish ceramic inlay from shell inlay.
- Najeonjang: AKS https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0067086 . Division of wood/lacquer/shell work; juleumjil shaped shell and kkeuneumjil short strips.
- Lee Hyung-man process/profile: Korea Heritage Agency https://www.kh.or.kr/brd/board/696/L/menu/314?bbIdx=110498&brdType=R&searchField=&searchText=&thisPage=1 . Includes process photographs and film transcript; training under Kim Bong-ryong, cutting, adhering, lacquering, revealing shell, polishing. Original images/film linked only.
- Embroidery: AKS https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0047963 . Material preparation, frame, drawing, stitching and finishing. Ieumsu outline; pyeongsu even filling stitches. Traditional finishing described as such, not prescribed as Charim production or care instructions.
- Bojagi: AKS https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0023523 . Wrapping/covering square cloth, construction categories; jogakbo joins fabric pieces. Do not call a single-piece Charim handkerchief jogakbo.
- Bookbinding: Seoul Metropolitan Archives https://archives.seoul.go.kr/post/1099 . Five-hole sewn binding. Historical principle distinguished from modern Charim hardcover design.

These sources are educational references. No referenced maker or institution is appointed to Charim. Cup glaze, pearl-like accents, hand/machine embroidery and hardcover production remain unconfirmed development choices. Summaries use English-first site copy with Korean technique names.
