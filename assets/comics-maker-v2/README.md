# Before the First Pour — Lessons with Teacher Cha

Version 2 extends the original pottery story with six new panels. The first six panels are retained at their original image paths; pages 04–06 add tea making, a first correction, repeated practice across harvests and Haein becoming an artisan years later.

Teacher Cha and Haein are fictional. The master–apprentice story is not an appointed supplier biography or a heritage certification claim. The tea scenes compress time and do not prescribe a production recipe. The site links to the separately sourced Ritual page for real Korean tea-making history.

English is the default reading language; all dialogue, narration and scene notes also have Korean text. Narrative lettering stays in accessible HTML rather than being baked into generated images.

Final cups preserve the approved minimal nacre flower and exterior foot accents. The original six ceramic-making panels remain available, along with their original art-direction file in assets/comics-maker-v1.

Generation mode: built-in image_gen. Full prompts and references are in art-direction.json. The source PNGs are retained here; responsive WebP files and archive copies are served from public/.
